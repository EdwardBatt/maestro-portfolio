# 1 — Registering a client and a brand

**Who this is for:** Account Managers (client services), Traffic and the Power Admin. Everyone
else sees the list read-only.

**Why it matters:** everything else hangs off the client record. The Campaign Builder and the
single-job door list clients and brands — they do not create them — and the **account manager**
you set here is who fills in on every campaign for that client, who approves its campaigns, and who
answers its deadline requests. The business profile you capture is inherited by every campaign
brief.

**What you need before you start**

- The client's name as it should appear on every list and report.
- Who their account manager is.
- Optionally: what they sell, their business type, and the e-commerce or economics detail the
  brief templates draw on. All of it can be filled in later.

---

## The steps

### 1. Open Clients

**Clients** in the sidebar. The table lists every client with their business type, brands,
campaigns and account manager. **Brands** sit below it.

*(Screenshot withheld from the public copy: The clients list)*

### 2. Register the client

Press **+ Register client**. The form opens.

*(Screenshot withheld from the public copy: The client form)*

- **Name** — required, unique.
- **Account manager** — the person who owns the relationship. Pick from the account managers.
- **Business type** — tick what applies.
- **Business profile** — what they sell, USP, digital presence, sales process, seasonality,
  best-sellers, lessons from past work. Optional, but every campaign brief for this client starts
  from it, so what you write here you do not write again.
- **E-commerce** and **economics** sections — for online sellers: catalogue size, order value,
  returns, daily capacity.
- **Odoo code** — the finance system's code for the client, when it exists.

*(Screenshot withheld from the public copy: The form, filled in)*

Press **Save**. The client appears in the list.

*(Screenshot withheld from the public copy: The client, registered)*

### 3. Add a brand

Below the clients table, under **Brands**: pick the client, type the brand name, press **+ Add**.
A client can have several brands; a campaign is for one of them (or for the client with no brand).

*(Screenshot withheld from the public copy: Adding a brand)*

*(Screenshot withheld from the public copy: The brand, added)*

### 4. Changing and removing

- **Edit** a client or brand with the pencil in its row.
- **Delete** with the bin. It asks once — the bin becomes a red *Really delete?*. A client or
  brand that is still in use (campaigns, jobs) cannot be deleted: *"Can't delete — it's still in
  use."*

---

## The rules

- **A client has one account manager.** Every campaign for the client defaults to them; the
  Campaign Builder lets you name a different one for a single campaign.
- **A client with no account manager** can still be registered, but its campaigns have nobody to
  approve them until one is set. The campaign page will say so.
- **Names are unique** — for clients across the agency, for brands within a client.
- **Who can register:** anyone with the client-services permission — account managers, Traffic,
  the Power Admin. Others see the list without the buttons.

## What happens next

- Create work for the client: a campaign (guide 2) or a single job (guide 3).
- The client's Library folders are created with its first campaign; the reference materials you
  file there are read by the AI when it writes briefs.

# Performance Brief - Baytk Furniture

**Brand / Product:** Baytk Home Furniture
**Market(s):** Baghdad, Erbil
**Brief date:** 03/02/2027

## Business snapshot

Baytk sells mid-range home furniture from two showrooms and an Instagram shop.
Most sales still close over WhatsApp after someone sees a post.

## Campaign objective

Drive qualified showroom visits and WhatsApp enquiries. Not brand awareness -
they need leads this quarter.

## Target audience

Newly married couples 25-35 furnishing a first home, plus families moving house.
Skews female for the initial enquiry, male for the final purchase decision.

## Geography and languages

Baghdad and Erbil only. Arabic primary, Kurdish for Erbil. Exclude anywhere we
cannot deliver.

## Offer

15% off any three-piece living room set through Ramadan, plus free delivery
inside Baghdad.

## Monthly budget

Between 13,000,000 and 26,000,000 IQD. Start at the lower end and scale what works.

## Platforms

Facebook and Instagram definitely. Client does not want TikTok - they think the
audience is too young. Google search if there is budget left.

## Targets and unit economics

Average order value around 900,000 IQD. Target cost per lead under 12,000 IQD.
They can handle about 20 serious enquiries a day before service suffers.

## Past learnings

Last agency ran Facebook lead ads and got volume but the leads were poor - lots
of people who just wanted the discount code. Quality over quantity this time.
